To stand out in development, you need to build and strengthen your professional presence online.

Here are some essential tips:

- **LinkedIn:** Keep your profile up to date with projects, certifications and experience.
- **GitHub:** Publish your repositories, contribute to open source projects and document your work well.
- **Web portfolio:** Create a website that showcases your best work with technical details.
- **Technical blog:** Share lessons, experiences, tutorials and personal projects.
- **Networking:** Attend events, webinars and meetups to expand your professional connections.

Remember that your personal brand can make a big difference between going unnoticed and attracting job opportunities.

Here is a quick example of how a portfolio could be structured:

```javascript
const portfolio = {
  name: 'Juan Pérez',
  role: 'Front-end Developer',
  skills: ['React', 'Next.js', 'TypeScript', 'TailwindCSS'],
  projects: [
    { name: 'Online Store', url: 'https://mi-tienda.com' },
    { name: 'Personal Blog', url: 'https://mi-blog.com' },
  ],
};
```
